Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026. Applies to all payments made by a School to School Office through our payment gateway partner, Razorpay.

1. Fee structure

School Office charges Schools under two separate heads of fee, the specific amounts of which are agreed individually with each School based on commercial negotiation and are not published as a fixed, one-size-fits-all price:

(a) Setup Fee — a one-time fee covering account creation, initial configuration, data migration assistance, and onboarding/training for the School's staff.

(b) Service Fee — the recurring or negotiated fee for continued access to and use of the School Office platform and its modules, as agreed with the specific School.

2. Setup Fee — non-refundable

The Setup Fee is non-refundable once paid, under all circumstances, including if a School subsequently decides not to continue using the platform, cancels its subscription, or does not complete onboarding. This is because the Setup Fee compensates School Office for work (account configuration, onboarding effort, and staff time) that is undertaken upon payment, regardless of the School's subsequent decision to continue or discontinue use of the service.

3. Service Fee — refundable within 15 days

The Service Fee is eligible for a refund if the School requests a refund within fifteen (15) days of the date the Service Fee payment was made. A request received after this fifteen (15) day window will not be eligible for a refund of the Service Fee, except where we agree to make an exception at our discretion.

To request a refund of the Service Fee, the School must contact us in writing (see Section 7) within the fifteen (15) day window, quoting the payment reference or Razorpay transaction ID, the date of payment, and the reason for the refund request.

4. Payment gateway

All payments to School Office are processed through Razorpay, a third-party payment gateway. We do not collect or store card, UPI, or net-banking credentials on our own servers; these are handled directly by Razorpay in accordance with its own security standards and applicable payment industry regulations. Any approved refund under this Policy will be processed back through Razorpay to the original payment method used for the transaction.

5. Refund processing time

Once a refund request is approved, we will initiate the refund through Razorpay within a reasonable period, generally within seven (7) business days of approval. The time for the refunded amount to actually reflect in the School's bank account or original payment method thereafter depends on Razorpay's own processing timelines and the School's bank or card issuer, and is typically an additional five (5) to seven (7) business days, though this may vary and is not controlled by School Office.

6. Cancellation of ongoing service

A School may cancel its ongoing use of the platform at any time by providing written notice to us. Cancellation will take effect from the end of the then-current paid service period, unless otherwise agreed. Cancelling ongoing service does not, by itself, entitle the School to a refund of any Service Fee already paid beyond what is provided for in Section 3, or to any refund of the Setup Fee under Section 2.

7. How to request a refund or cancellation

To request a refund or cancel your service, please write to us at support@schooloffice.tech with your school name, the payment/transaction reference, and the nature of your request. We will acknowledge your request and respond with the outcome within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with a revised effective date and will apply to payments made after that date; it will not retroactively alter the refund eligibility of a payment already made under the previous version of this Policy.

9. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Subject to applicable law, the courts at Ajmer, Rajasthan, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Policy.

10. Contact us

For any questions about this Policy, please contact us at support@schooloffice.tech or through our Contact page.
