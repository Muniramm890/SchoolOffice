Privacy & Data Security Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026. Applies to every school, administrator, teacher, student and parent account using the School Office platform at schooloffice.tech and admin.schooloffice.tech.

Plain-language summary (this does not replace the full policy below): We do not sell or rent your data to advertisers or data brokers. We use it only to run and improve School Office. We store it on Microsoft Azure in the Asia region. We take reasonable security measures, but no online service can guarantee absolute security or absolute, irreversible deletion of data — so we strongly recommend every school keep its own independent backup of its records.

1. Scope and who this policy covers

This Privacy & Data Security Policy ("Policy") is issued by the operator of School Office ("School Office", "we", "us", "our"), a school management software product accessible at schooloffice.tech and the associated application at admin.schooloffice.tech (together, the "Platform"). It applies to all personal data processed through the Platform, including data relating to the schools that subscribe to our service ("Schools"), their administrators, teachers and other staff, and the students and parents/guardians whose records a School maintains on the Platform (together, "Users" or "Data Principals", as the context requires).

This Policy is intended to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made thereunder (including the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, to the extent still applicable), and other applicable law in India. It should be read together with our Terms of Service and our Disclaimer, each of which forms part of the agreement between a School and School Office. If there is any direct conflict between this Policy and the Terms of Service specifically on a matter of data protection, this Policy will govern for that matter.

This Policy does not apply to any third-party website, application or service that may be linked from the Platform, or to any data a School collects or processes outside the Platform through its own separate systems, registers or paper records.

2. Definitions

"Personal Data" means data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, whether that identification is direct (such as a name or roll number) or indirect (such as a combination of attendance pattern and class section that, together, could identify a specific student), as defined under the DPDP Act.

"Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, recording, storage, use, sharing, disclosure, retrieval, and erasure, whether by automated or manual means.

"Data Fiduciary" means the person who alone or in conjunction with others determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. In the relationship this Policy describes, each School is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data of its own students, parents and staff that it enters into the Platform, because the School decides what data to collect, from whom, and why.

"Data Processor" means a person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary. School Office acts as a Data Processor for student, parent and staff data that a School enters into the Platform. We process such data strictly to provide the features the School has activated, on the School's instructions, and we do not repurpose that data for our own independent commercial ends.

"Data Principal" means the individual to whom the personal data relates — a student, parent, teacher or other staff member — and, where the individual is a child, this term includes their parent or lawful guardian acting on the child's behalf, consistent with the DPDP Act.

"Sub-processor" means any third-party service provider that we engage to process personal data on our behalf as part of operating the Platform, such as our cloud hosting provider.

3. Responsibilities of the School as Data Fiduciary

Because the School controls what personal data is entered into School Office and for what purpose, primary legal responsibility for the lawfulness of that data collection rests with the School as Data Fiduciary. Specifically, the School is responsible for:

(a) obtaining all consents required under applicable law before entering personal data into the Platform — including, where the Data Principal is a child, verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, obtained in the manner required under Section 9 of the DPDP Act;

(b) identifying and relying on a valid lawful basis (such as consent, or a legitimate educational or administrative purpose recognised under applicable law) before sharing student, parent and staff personal data with School Office;

(c) ensuring the accuracy, completeness and currency of the data it enters or uploads, and promptly correcting or removing data that is inaccurate or no longer needed;

(d) informing its own students, parents and staff about how their personal data will be processed through the Platform — including by sharing or linking to this Policy — and about who they may contact at the School with questions;

(e) restricting access to the School's own account and login credentials to authorised staff only, and promptly notifying us if it suspects unauthorised access to its account.

School Office processes personal data only on the documented or reasonably inferred instructions of the School and strictly for the purpose of operating the Platform's modules that the School has chosen to use; we do not independently decide to collect additional categories of student or staff data beyond what the School itself configures and enters.

4. Information we collect

Account and school information: the school's registered name, address, contact telephone number and email, GST or Udyam registration details where provided, and the names, designations, email addresses and login credentials of the administrators and staff who set up and operate the account on the School's behalf.

Student and academic records: the data that the School itself chooses to enter, upload or generate within the Platform, which may include, depending on which modules the School activates: student names, roll numbers, class and section, date of birth, parent/guardian names and contact details, photographs uploaded by the School for identification purposes, attendance records, examination and assessment scores, report cards, fee ledgers and payment status, transport and route assignments, and homework or learning-management submissions.

Usage and log data: technical information collected automatically when the Platform is used, such as IP address, browser type and version, device and operating system information, pages or screens accessed, actions taken within the application, and timestamps of activity. This data is collected to operate, secure, debug and improve the Platform, and is not used to build advertising profiles of students or staff.

Cookies and similar technologies: we may use strictly necessary cookies or local storage to keep a user logged in and to remember basic preferences within a session. We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on the Platform.

Communications: any information a School, administrator, or prospective customer shares with us directly — for example, through our inquiry form, a support email, or a phone call — including the content of that communication and any attachments provided.

5. How we use information

We process personal data only for the following, specifically stated purposes, and for no other purpose without further notice:

(a) to provide, operate, host and maintain the School Office Platform and the specific modules that the relevant School has activated, including generating reports, notifications and dashboards from the data the School has entered;

(b) for limited internal use to maintain and improve our services — for example, aggregated analysis of which features are used most, identifying and diagnosing errors or performance bottlenecks, load and capacity planning, and prioritising which improvements to build next. Wherever reasonably practicable for this purpose, we prefer to work with aggregated or de-identified usage patterns rather than individually identifiable student records;

(c) to provide customer support, respond to queries, and communicate service-related notices (such as maintenance windows or security notices) to School administrators;

(d) to detect, investigate and address technical issues, suspected fraud, unauthorised access attempts, or other misuse of the Platform;

(e) to comply with an applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid and enforceable request from a government or regulatory authority, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell, rent, lease or trade personal data to any third party for that third party's own marketing, profiling or advertising purposes, and we do not share student data with advertisers or data brokers, under any circumstance. Any future change permitting a new category of use or sharing would require an update to this Policy and, where legally required, fresh notice or consent.

6. Sharing of information

Consistent with Section 5 above, we do not sell personal data. We disclose or share personal data only in the following limited circumstances, and only to the extent reasonably necessary for that purpose:

(a) with sub-processors who provide infrastructure or services necessary to run the Platform — at present, this is limited to our cloud hosting provider, Microsoft Azure, described further in Section 7, and, where a School uses an integrated payment option, the relevant payment processor engaged for that purpose;

(b) where required to comply with a valid legal obligation, a binding court order, or a lawful request from a government or regulatory authority, in which case we will disclose only the data specifically requested and, where legally permitted, will make reasonable efforts to notify the affected School;

(c) to protect the rights, property, or safety of School Office, our Users, or members of the public, where we believe in good faith that disclosure is reasonably necessary to prevent harm, fraud, or a violation of our Terms of Service;

(d) with the School's own authorised users, strictly as directed by the School's own configuration and use of the Platform — for example, a class teacher viewing attendance that a parent submitted through the Platform, or an accountant viewing fee records for their assigned class;

(e) in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of some or all of our business assets, in which case we will require the receiving party to continue to honour the commitments made in this Policy, or will provide notice of any material change before data is transferred.

7. Where and how data is stored

School Office data is hosted on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure. We have configured our hosting environment to use Azure's Asia geographic region setting, which is intended to keep data within data centres that Microsoft designates as part of that region. Azure operates multiple physical data centres within this broader region and allocates specific facilities, redundancy, and failover arrangements as part of its own infrastructure management practices; the precise physical data centre location within the Asia region, and any internal replication Microsoft performs for its own operational or disaster-recovery purposes, is managed by Microsoft Azure and is not something School Office independently discloses, selects at a granular level, or controls.

Azure's own security safeguards, third-party compliance certifications, and any further sub-processors Microsoft itself engages, apply to the underlying infrastructure layer, in addition to the application-level measures we describe in Section 8. Users seeking further detail on Microsoft Azure's own data protection commitments may refer to Microsoft's published trust and compliance documentation, which is maintained independently of School Office.

8. Security measures

We apply reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, having regard to the nature of the data and the resources reasonably available to a business of our size. These measures may include: encryption of data in transit between a user's browser and our servers; role-based access controls limiting which members of our team can access production data, and only to the extent necessary for their role; unique login credentials for School administrators and staff, rather than shared logins; and monitoring aimed at identifying unusual or unauthorised access patterns.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot and do not guarantee absolute security of any information transmitted to, or stored on, the Platform or on our sub-processors' infrastructure. Schools are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their own login credentials and for promptly changing credentials and notifying us if they suspect a compromise.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to affect Data Principals, we will assess the breach, take reasonable steps to contain and remediate it, and provide notification to the affected School and, where required, to the Data Protection Board of India, in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act and rules made thereunder.

9. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal data for as long as the relevant School's account remains active and the data continues to serve the purpose for which it was collected, and for a further reasonable period thereafter as needed to comply with our legal obligations (such as financial record-keeping requirements), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Upon a valid, verified request from the School to delete specific data, or upon lawful termination of the School's account, we will make reasonable commercial efforts to delete or irreversibly anonymise the relevant personal data from our primary, active production systems within a reasonable period, generally targeted within ninety (90) days of the verified request, subject to any legal retention obligation that requires us to retain specific records for longer.

Notwithstanding the above, we do not represent, warrant or guarantee the complete, absolute or immediate erasure of data from every system, backup, cache, archival copy or log file. As is standard practice for cloud-hosted applications, routine backups are taken for disaster-recovery purposes and are rotated out over time according to our backup cycle; residual copies of deleted data may therefore persist in backup systems for a limited additional period until that cycle completes, or where retention is otherwise required by law, even after the data has been removed from active, user-facing systems.

Because complete data deletion across every system cannot be guaranteed by any cloud-hosted platform, and because no platform can guarantee uninterrupted, error-free availability at all times, we strongly recommend that every School independently maintain and periodically update its own backup of all student, staff, attendance, fee and academic records entered into School Office — for example, by using the Platform's own export functionality where available, or by maintaining parallel institutional records as many schools already do for regulatory purposes. School Office is a tool to help manage this data more efficiently; it is not a substitute for a School's own statutory record-keeping and backup obligations under applicable education, taxation or administrative regulations, and a School should not treat the Platform as its sole or permanent system of record.

10. Children's personal data

School Office is used by schools to manage data relating to students, many of whom will be children as defined under the DPDP Act. Consistent with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, and to give practical effect to the child-protection intent of that section:

(a) the School, as Data Fiduciary, is responsible for identifying which of its students are children under applicable law and for obtaining verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before that child's personal data is first entered into the Platform, in whatever manner is required or recognised under the DPDP Act and any rules issued under it;

(b) School Office does not itself undertake behavioural monitoring or tracking of children across other websites or services, and does not serve targeted advertising directed at children through the Platform, and has no intention of introducing advertising of any kind directed at student accounts;

(c) features that could involve any processing likely to cause detrimental effect to the well-being of a child are not knowingly built into the Platform; if a School identifies such a concern with any feature, it should raise it with our Grievance Officer under Section 12;

(d) by entering a child's personal data into School Office, the School represents and warrants to us that it has obtained the consents, and satisfied the notice requirements, that applicable law requires of it as Data Fiduciary before doing so.

11. Rights of Data Principals

Students, parents and staff whose personal data is processed through School Office may have rights under the DPDP Act, including, subject to the conditions and exceptions set out in that Act, the right to obtain a summary of the personal data being processed and the processing activities undertaken, the right to seek correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, and the right to seek erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless retention is otherwise required by law.

Because the School is the Data Fiduciary for student, parent and staff data entered into the Platform, such requests should ordinarily be directed to the relevant School's administration in the first instance, as the School is best placed to verify the identity of the requester and to determine what data it holds and why. School Office will provide reasonable technical assistance to a School in responding to such verified requests, in our capacity as Data Processor, including by providing export or deletion functionality within the Platform where available, or by carrying out a request the School directs us to fulfil on its behalf.

Where a Data Principal contacts School Office directly rather than their School, we will, where reasonably practicable, direct them to the relevant School, and may separately assist the School in addressing the request.
