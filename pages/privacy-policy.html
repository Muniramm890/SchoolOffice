Privacy & Data Security Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026. Applies to every school, administrator, teacher, student and parent account using the School Office platform at schooloffice.tech and admin.schooloffice.tech.

Plain-language summary (this does not replace the full policy below): We do not sell or rent your data to advertisers or data brokers. We use it only to run and improve School Office. We store it on Microsoft Azure in the Asia region. We take reasonable security measures, but no online service can guarantee absolute security or absolute, irreversible deletion of data — so we strongly recommend every school keep its own independent backup of its records.

1. Scope and who this policy covers

This Privacy & Data Security Policy ("Policy") is issued by the operator of School Office ("School Office", "we", "us", "our"), a school management software product. It applies to all personal data processed through the School Office platform, including data relating to schools, their administrators and staff, students, and parents/guardians (together, "Users").

This Policy is intended to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made thereunder, and other applicable law in India. It should be read together with our Terms of Service and Disclaimer.

2. Definitions

"Personal Data" means data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, as defined under the DPDP Act.

"Data Fiduciary" means the person who determines the purpose and means of processing personal data — in this relationship, the School is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data of its own students, parents and staff that it enters into School Office.

"Data Processor" means a person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary — School Office acts as a Data Processor for student, parent and staff data that a School enters into the platform, and processes such data strictly on the School's instructions and for the purpose of providing the School Office service.

"Data Principal" means the individual to whom the personal data relates (a student, parent, teacher or staff member), and, where the individual is a child, includes their parent or lawful guardian.

3. Responsibilities of the School as Data Fiduciary

Because the School controls what personal data is entered into School Office and why, the School is responsible for:

(a) obtaining all consents required under applicable law — including verifiable parental or guardian consent for the processing of any child's personal data — before entering such data into the platform;

(b) ensuring it has a lawful basis to share student, parent and staff personal data with School Office for the purpose of using the service;

(c) the accuracy of the data it enters, and for informing its own students, parents and staff about how their data will be used through School Office, including by sharing or linking to this Policy.

School Office processes personal data only on the instructions of the School and for the purpose of operating the platform, and does not independently determine why student or staff data is collected.

4. Information we collect

Account and school information: school name, address, contact details, and login credentials of administrators and staff who set up and operate the account.

Student and academic records: data that the School enters or uploads into the platform, which may include student names, roll numbers, attendance, grades, exam results, fee records, and related academic or administrative information.

Usage and log data: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and timestamps, collected automatically to operate, secure and troubleshoot the platform.

Communications: information you share with us when you contact support, request a demo, or otherwise communicate with us.

5. How we use information

We use personal data only for the following purposes:

(a) to provide, operate and maintain the School Office platform and its modules for the School that entered the data;

(b) for limited internal use to maintain and improve our services — for example, understanding which features are used, diagnosing errors, and improving reliability and performance. We do not use personal data to build advertising profiles.

(c) to provide customer support and respond to queries;

(d) to detect, prevent and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse of the platform;

(e) to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes. We do not share student data with advertisers.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We share personal data only in the following limited circumstances:

(a) with sub-processors who provide infrastructure or services necessary to run the platform — currently, this is limited to our cloud hosting provider, Microsoft Azure (see Section 7);

(b) where required to comply with a valid legal obligation, court order, or request from a government or regulatory authority;

(c) to protect the rights, property or safety of School Office, our Users, or the public, where permitted by law;

(d) with the School's own authorised users (for example, a teacher viewing attendance a parent submitted), as directed by the School's own use of the platform.

7. Where and how data is stored

School Office data is hosted on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure. We have configured our hosting to use Azure's Asia geographic region. Azure operates multiple data centres within this region and allocates specific facilities as part of its own infrastructure management; the exact physical data centre location within this region is managed by Microsoft Azure and is not something School Office independently discloses or controls. Azure's own security, compliance certifications and sub-processor practices apply to the underlying infrastructure, in addition to the measures we apply at the application level.

8. Security measures

We apply reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, which may include encryption of data in transit, access controls limiting who within our team can access production data, and monitoring for unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of any information transmitted to or stored on the platform. In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to affect Data Principals or the Data Protection Board of India, we will provide notification as required under the DPDP Act.

9. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal data for as long as the School's account remains active, and for a reasonable period thereafter as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Upon a valid request from the School to delete data, or upon termination of the School's account, we will make reasonable efforts to delete or anonymise the relevant personal data from our active systems within a reasonable period. However, we do not represent or guarantee complete, absolute or irreversible erasure of data from all systems, backups, caches or logs — residual copies may persist in backup systems for a limited period as part of standard backup cycles, or where retention is required by law.

Because complete data deletion and continuous data availability cannot be guaranteed by any online platform, we strongly recommend that every School independently maintain and periodically update its own backup of all student, staff and academic records entered into School Office. School Office is not a substitute for a School's own record-keeping and backup obligations under applicable education and administrative regulations.

10. Children's personal data

School Office is used by schools to manage data relating to students who may be children under the DPDP Act. Consistent with Section 9 of the DPDP Act:

(a) the School, as Data Fiduciary, is responsible for obtaining verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before a child's personal data is entered into the platform;

(b) School Office does not undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring of children, and does not serve targeted advertising directed at children, through the platform;

(c) by entering a child's data into School Office, the School represents to us that it has obtained the consents required under applicable law to do so.

11. Rights of Data Principals

Students, parents and staff whose personal data is processed through School Office may have rights under the DPDP Act, including the right to access information about how their personal data is processed, and to seek correction, completion, updating or erasure of their personal data. Because the School is the Data Fiduciary for this data, such requests should ordinarily be directed to the relevant School in the first instance. School Office will provide reasonable assistance to the School in responding to such verified requests, in our capacity as Data Processor.
